Name Game: Mutual Friends

Find the friends you and another person both know.

Need help? Email ebhmacy@mac.com.

How it works

  • Two people stand near each other, each with the app open on an iPhone.
  • One taps Show QR code. The other taps Scan QR code and scans it.
  • The two phones connect over Bluetooth and privately compare their contacts. Each of you sees only the people you have in common.

Nothing is uploaded. The app has no accounts and no servers. See the privacy policy for the details.

If something doesn't work

  • Check the permissions. The app needs Contacts, Bluetooth and Camera. Open Settings, then Name Game, and make sure all three are on.
  • Turn Bluetooth on. If it's off, swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center and turn it on.
  • Stay close. Keep the two phones in the same room until the result appears.
  • Use a fresh code. Each QR code works once and expires after a short time. Tap Show QR code again to get a new one.
  • Update the app. Both phones should have the latest version.
  • Phone numbers. The app compares US and Canadian phone numbers only. Today only iPhones can show a code. Android phones can scan.

Send me a problem report

In the app, open People I hide, then Advanced, then Share a problem report. It opens the iPhone share sheet so you can email it to me. The report never contains names, phone numbers or contacts, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.
